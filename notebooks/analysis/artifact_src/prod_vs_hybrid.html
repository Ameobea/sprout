Anime Atlas · prod vs hybrid · 2026-08-04

The live model and its hybrid challenger, side by side

One document, two columns: the model serving production today against the concat-graft hybrid now selectable from the app's model dropdown. Every offline measurement we have, re-cut as a direct comparison under matched conditions — same eval users, same corruption seeds, same serving math, same filters. The decision rationale lives in the production decision artifact; this is the scorecard.

Prod — "Aug. 2026" (2026-logq)

The live checkpoint (jax_model_fresh_logq): 2-channel DAE, 512-d bottleneck, dual heads, logQ-lift serving through the (α,k) niche knob. Trained on the full Aug 2026 dataset.

Hybrid — "Aug. 2026 Hybrid (Beta)" (2026-hybrid)

Same architecture plus a 256-d projection of the frozen closed-form EASE score vector concatenated into the presence decoder. Currently the probe checkpoint (trained on 90% of users) — the full-data retrain is expected to only help its column.

5 · The presence/rating default is on the wrong side of its own curve

The app's Presence/Rating slider (default 0.4) does not reach the server as 0.4: it passes through a deliberate cubic remap, lweff = ⅔·s³ + ⅓·s + 0.01, chosen to stretch the rating-heavy end. The consequence, measured only now: the default slider position serves at effective lw ≈ 0.19 — 81% of the score's weight on the rating head, which every probe this cycle has identified as the weaker-signal head. Both independent evals agree on where the curve actually peaks:

Slider position → effective lw → outcome

both evals shown at the served (effective) lw · hybrid model

  • What the curves actually say: temporal overall is flat within its noise floor from effective 0.15 to 0.4 — no harm moving right. PTW overall peaks at effective 0.4–0.5, about +2pp filtered r@250 over the as-served position — on that eval the move is worth roughly as much as the architecture upgrade. Both hidden-gems mid bands rise monotonically toward presence-heavy mixes (temporal mid +25–30% relative from 0.19 → 0.4). And the rating-heavy end is a cliff: lw = 0 ranks intent items at median ≈ 1,270 vs ≈ 310 — every step toward it costs.
  • Decided and implemented (2026-08-04): the default slider moved 0.4 → 0.6 (lands at effective 0.354, near the PTW peak, mid bands close to their ceiling, temporal overall unchanged within noise) — DEFAULT_LOGIT_WEIGHT in conf.ts, used by the client params, the URL serializer, and the SSR loader. The remap itself is unchanged; slider 0 still means lw = 0.01 and slider 1 means 1.01 — pinning the endpoints remains open.
  • Non-raters now score presence-only (also implemented): the slider was already hidden for them, but scoring still used the default mix — an acknowledged oversight from when the control was hidden. The server now forces effective lw = 1 exactly whenever the profile is a non-rater, regardless of the request's weight; verified end-to-end (a non-rater profile returns identical lists at any slider position, a rated profile still responds to it).
  • This is a serving-config change, not a retrain — orthogonal to the hybrid decision, and it benefits prod today. Both models move together across the whole sweep (the hybrid's edge is lw-independent), so the two decisions don't interact.

6 · The rating side is a tie — by construction, and now by measurement

The graft touches only the presence path: the rating decoder reads the bottleneck alone in both models, and the EASE matrix contains no rating data. Prod's rating head measures 0.447 z-MAE on dropped items — better than every closed-form item-item alternative measured (best: 0.492), which is why no EASE graft exists on this side. The hybrid retrains the same head jointly, and the PTW eval now confirms the tie directly: ranking by each model's own rating head alone (lw = 0), the two are indistinguishable ( recall@250). The open question on this side was never the head — it's the weight the serving score gives it, section 5.

A worked example of view-vs-app divergence: City The Animation (pop rank 2,189) sits mid-pack here for the hybrid but reaches the app's top ranks, and the gap decomposes cleanly — the served knob + lw move it from ~32 to ~16 on the older profile, one newly added high-affinity rating (Yani Neko, 10/10, watching) moves it to ~4, and the media-type filters remove most of what outranks it. The offline lists answer a different question than the app screen; both are correct.
