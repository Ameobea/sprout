The hybrid recommender: decision and ship plan

Phase 0 is complete: every open question from the four audit rounds has a measured answer, each load-bearing result is confirmed on a second seed, and one architecture dominates every frontier we can compute offline. This document assumes the hybrid NN+EASE direction is the decision and organizes the evidence and the path to production around it. Background and full history: the information-theoretic audit artifact (rounds 1–4).

Decision: retrain with the post-bottleneck EASE graft (concat), serve with an optional light EASE-lift stack.

The model becomes the prod architecture plus a 256-d projection of the frozen closed-form EASE score vector concatenated to the presence decoder's input — one jointly-trained network, confirmed across two seeds, cold-start-safe by construction, with no franchise inflation. The serving score optionally stacks z-normed EASE-lift on the graft's logits (w ≈ 0.2–0.35, off below ~64 items) through the existing (α,k) knob — that stack strictly dominates every other configuration measured, including the original blend-on-NN, on both mid-band and deep-tail recall at matched overall. The fallback if the retrain surprises: the blend-on-NN ships without touching training and banks most of the win.

What the guardrail says

  • No regression anywhere. Both grafts sit within the harness noise floor of the NN on every overall metric; concat improves median rank (371 vs 409).
  • The hybrid win transfers to future watches. Mid-band recall@50 goes 0.043 (NN) → 0.046 (concat) → 0.074 (blend), +72%, and survives the franchise filter — it is not sequel prediction.
  • EASE alone fails the product task (mid-band 0.014–0.019, below the NN, with top-10 lists at popularity rank ~130–200) — the hybrid is not "more EASE", it is EASE's pairwise detail with the NN's generalization.
  • lw = 0.3 is re-validated as the overall optimum; mid-band keeps rising toward logits-heavy mixes — the rating head's pull is head-tier-biased.

4 · The rating head: independent, and staying that way

The rating decoder reads only the bottleneck in every graft variant, and B contains no rating data. Could EASE-style item-item structure help ratings the way it helps presence? Measured answer: no — the asymmetry is real. The best closed-form item-item rating predictor (rating-correlation kNN, top-20 neighbors) trails the NN head, and the gap widens at full context (0.477 vs 0.436) — exactly opposite the presence side, where EASE dominates at full context. The NN already extracts more rating signal than item-item space holds.

6 · Where this sits in the literature

A dedicated review (scratchpad lit_review.md, 20+ sources) puts the verdict at partially novel: the family is established, the specific mechanism is not.

  • Closest prior art: VASP (Vančura & Kordík 2021) — a "Neural EASE" trained jointly in parallel with a deep Mult-VAE, fused post-bottleneck by element-wise product. Their ablation (joint 0.448 > post-hoc ensemble 0.442 NDCG@100) independently supports grafting over blending. Differences: our EASE path is the frozen closed form, fusion is an additive gated residual, and the gate learns profile-size adaptivity — none published.
  • The gate rediscovered 20 years of blending practice: Netflix-Prize teams learned blend weights per support bin (BigChaos, 15 bins); LINK (SIGIR 2025) confirms the complementarity is popularity-structured — linear wins co-occurrence/less-popular, neural wins popular/complex. Our gate learned the same curve by SGD.
  • Steck predicted the bottleneck result: the EASE paper attributes exactly this failure to "hidden layers with limited capacity", and Jin et al. (KDD 2021) give the singular-value account of what low-rank maps destroy. Our contribution is the controlled width sweep + the input-vs-output injection comparison, which nobody reports.
  • Actionable upgrades from the review: RDLAE (SIGIR 2023) fixes EASE's ridge-induced dead tail at the source (relaxed diagonal); DAN (SIGIR 2025) generalizes our s−μ lift with big reported tail gains; higher-order EASE (Steck & Liang 2021) adds triplet interactions — comfortable at a 6k corpus and the natural place for anti-sequel corrections. Also worth an ablation someday: unfreezing B inside the graft (VASP's joint-training result suggests more win), and multiplicative fusion.
  • Franchise magnetism and small-profile popularity degeneration are essentially undocumented — closest is Cremonesi et al. 2010's "trivial recommendation" exclusion. Our characterization is ahead of the literature there.

7 · Ship plan

Two phases, gates between them. Everything here is standard cycle machinery — no new research required.

Phase 1 — candidate build + dev-server eyeballing

  1. Final training run: concat graft on the full dataset (all users), prod recipe, B rebuilt from the same vectors and versioned with the checkpoint. Optional second run: both variant (marginally better battery numbers, +1 Dense).
  2. Artifacts: B at bf16 (~72 MB — quantization delta check vs f32 scores), μ vector, popularity counts (already shipped per the logQ migration).
  3. Rust server: EASE channel = sparse row-gather + sum over B (~0.6M MACs at median profile — negligible vs 50ms P95), per-request z-norm, decoder input widened by the projection. Stack scoring behind a dev flag: + w·z(liftEASE) with w gated on profile size.
  4. In-app eyeballing on the dev server — the gate this phase exists for: real profiles, real filters (incl. ONA/OVA, which offline eval never modeled), knob sweep by hand.

Phase 2 — calibration + deploy gates

  1. Knob re-anchor: ρ(logits, pop) ≈ +0.30 says the remap transfers; still re-fit the linearization on the final scorer's own frontier (alpha_k_sweep methodology), re-derive the niche-end α floor, decide the stack's w default.
  2. Golden regen + bf16-sim eval gate (existing harness, ~40 min).
  3. LOO + temporal guardrails on the final checkpoint (serve-prior mode), judged against the frozen anchors and noise floors.
  4. B lifecycle: closed-form rebuild each data cycle alongside vectorization — minutes of compute, versioned like weights.

Known risks, stated

  • Probes trained on 90% of users at 512-d — the full-data retrain is expected ≈identical (memorization nil) but is itself the confirmation.
  • Offline filter replica covers season relations only; ONA/OVA filter and metadata gaps land on dev-server eyeballs.
  • The temporal task is sequel-heavy (30% franchise) — hybrid wins there anyway, but the stratified+filtered views are the ones to trust.
